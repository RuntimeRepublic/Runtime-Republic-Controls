Version: 0.1

Status: Draft

---

# 1. Purpose

Sets how the founder keeps open office hours for members, teams and organizations.

---

# 2. Core Principle

A regular, predictable time when anyone can get help.

---

# 3. Rules

1. Office hours are held weekly on Discord first.
2. Other platforms (for example, Facebook or YouTube) are added only if the same video feed can reach them. Smoke-test before announcing.
3. The day and time are posted on the join page and the Runtime Republic Facebook Page.
4. Office hours teach and answer questions. No product demonstrations (CONTROL 06).
5. Never show a member's private finances on screen.
