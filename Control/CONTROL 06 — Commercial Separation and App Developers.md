CONTROL 06 — Commercial Separation and App Developers

Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-11-15
Status: Draft
Project: Runtime Republic

---

# 1. Purpose

Keeps Runtime Republic free and neutral while encouraging any developer to build software that saves labor for organizations, teams and people.

---

# 2. Core Principle

Everything in Runtime Republic is free, and no app is ever required.

---

# 3. Rules

## 3.1 Lean Office Pro

1. Lean Office Pro appears only as one labeled footer link and on its own website.
2. It never appears in office hours, team repositories or the directory, except as one entry on the app-developer list.
3. Its offers are labeled as Lean Office Pro, never presented as Runtime Republic advice.

## 3.2 App developers

1. Runtime Republic encourages any developer to find needs of organizations, teams and people and fill them.
2. The app-developer list is neutral: the same rules for every developer, alphabetical order, and Lean Office Pro's ownership of Runtime Republic disclosed on its entry.
3. A public needs board collects the work that eats people's time, drawn from friction logs. Developers may use it freely.
4. Gov data repositories are public and free for any app to use.

## 3.3 Rules every developer follows, including Lean Office Pro

1. No app is ever required to take part in anything.
2. No access to private repositories beyond what a user grants their own app.
3. No claim of Runtime Republic endorsement.
4. Member data is never sold or mined.
5. Membership, team status and qualification never depend on buying anything.

---

# 4. Edge Cases

| Case | Handling |
|---|---|
| A developer claims endorsement | Removed from the list. |
| An app breaks a privacy rule | Removed from the list; members told. |
| A member asks which app to use | Point to the list. Runtime Republic does not recommend one. |
